Second

A personal execution agent. You say what you want. It plans the route, puts the work in your calendar, watches what actually happened, and works out why when something slips.

Second does everything it can do without you, then hands you the smallest possible piece. It stays silent unless a decision is genuinely yours to make.

What it does

  • Turns spoken goals into a ladder — from a lifetime ambition down to something that fits in a calendar slot on Tuesday.
  • Schedules across competing goals against your real calendar.
  • Checks each morning what happened yesterday, and asks you directly when the calendar cannot know.
  • Diagnoses a slip with quoted evidence, or says UNKNOWN and asks. It does not guess at motivation.
  • Changes the plan when the plan was the problem — a slot that keeps losing to a standing meeting gets moved, not postponed.
  • Prepares work up to the last click. It drafts email; it never sends it.

What it will not do

  • Send a message on your behalf.
  • Delete a calendar event.
  • Move a meeting you do not own.
  • State a cause it cannot quote evidence for.

Status

A demonstration project, built for the AWS Agents for Humans hackathon. It runs against a single developer account. It is not a product, there is no sign-up, and it is not accepting users.
