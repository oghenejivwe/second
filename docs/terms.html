What this is

Second is a demonstration project built for the AWS Agents for Humans hackathon. It is not a commercial product. There is no sign-up, no account system, and no support commitment. It runs against a single developer's own Google account.

Use at your own risk

The software is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. It writes to a calendar and creates email drafts. It may schedule things badly, misread evidence, or be wrong about why something did not happen. Check what it does before relying on it.

Your data

How the software handles data it reads from Google is described in the privacy policy. You can withdraw access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
